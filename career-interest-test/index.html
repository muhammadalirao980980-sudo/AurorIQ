About this instrument

What this measures — and what it doesn't

  • Measures

    Vocational interests

    The kinds of work and activity that draw you, across Holland's six RIASEC themes. It captures what tends to energize you — a genuinely useful and stable input to thinking about career direction.

  • Method

    30-item RIASEC inventory

    Five items per theme, each rated on a five-point like–dislike scale, presented in shuffled order to reduce patterning. Scores are normalized to a percentage per theme and ranked into your Holland Code.

  • Result

    Code + ranked profile

    Your three-letter Holland Code, a full ranking of all six themes with scores, and illustrative career families for your top three — framed as directions to explore, not a shortlist.

  • Limits

    Honest limits

    Interest is not ability, and a code is not a destiny. This can't see your skills, values, or circumstances, and the career lists are generic examples. It's a self-reflection map, never a hiring or aptitude tool.

Questions

Before you begin

What is a Holland Code?

A three-letter summary of your strongest work interests, drawn from the six RIASEC themes — Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, Conventional. A code like IAS means your top interests are Investigative, Artistic, and Social, in that order. It's a shorthand for the kinds of work that tend to energize you.

Does this test measure ability?

No — it measures interest, not aptitude. It tells you what kinds of activity you're drawn to, not how skilled you are or would become. Interest and ability often overlap, but not always, so treat your top themes as directions worth exploring rather than proof you'd excel at them.

How should I use my results?

As a starting map. Your top themes point to families of work worth investigating through real exploration — shadowing, side projects, or talking to people who do the job. The career lists are illustrative examples of each theme, not personalized recommendations or a ranked shortlist.

Is my data saved anywhere?

No. The entire test runs in your browser. Your answers never leave your device and nothing is stored or sent to a server. Refreshing the page clears everything.
