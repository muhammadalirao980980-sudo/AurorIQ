Under the hood

The engine behind the instruments

No black boxes. This is exactly how your result is computed — and where it lives.

  • 3PL IRT

    Adaptive item selection

    A three-parameter item-response model re-estimates your ability after every answer and picks the next question where it carries maximum information.

  • Age-normed

    Fair to your age band

    Percentiles are adjusted along a fluid-reasoning curve, so a 19-year-old and a 60-year-old are each compared to their true peers.

  • 95% CI

    Honest uncertainty

    Every estimate ships with its confidence interval. A number without its error bars is flattery — we report both.

  • On-device

    Private by architecture

    There is no server scoring your answers. The engine runs in your browser and results are stored on your device — nowhere else.

Questions

Before you begin

Is the test free?

Yes. The full assessment and your complete result are free, with no account or email required.

How long does it take?

About 15 minutes. You'll answer 25 questions that adapt in difficulty as you go.

How accurate is an online IQ test?

AurorIQ produces a percentile-based estimate using an adaptive item-response engine. It's a tool for reflection and curiosity — not a clinical assessment. Only a proctored test administered by a licensed psychologist using a validated instrument such as the WAIS-IV qualifies for clinical, educational, or legal purposes.

What is a cognitive archetype?

It describes the shape of your profile — which reasoning domains are strongest relative to the others. Two people with the same overall score can be different archetypes.

Can I use my score for Mensa?

No. AurorIQ scores can't be used for Mensa or any clinical, educational, or legal purpose. Those require a supervised assessment with a validated instrument.

What is AurorIQ?

AurorIQ is an intelligence platform — a growing set of free, science-grounded instruments that measure how you think. The flagship is an adaptive IQ test; personality testing lives on our sister instrument Personica, with learning, career, and productivity instruments in development.

Do you require an account or sell my data?

No account, no email, and no sale of your answers. The assessment is scored in your browser and your results are stored on your own device.
