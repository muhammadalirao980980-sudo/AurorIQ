About AurorIQ

Privacy Policy

Updated September 18, 2026

At a glance

AurorIQ assessments run in your browser and do not require a test account or email address. Saved progress and results are stored on your device where supported. Optional Google Analytics loads only after you accept cookies on the public AurorIQ domain. Advertising is currently inactive in this version.

Assessment data and browser storage

The scoring engine does not send your individual answers to an AurorIQ assessment server. Browser storage supports saved IQ progress, result history, theme and cookie preferences; some tools keep their state only while the page is open. Persistent entries remain until overwritten or removed using your browser’s site-data settings. Closing a tab does not necessarily delete saved results.

On a shared device, another person using the same browser profile may be able to view saved results. Downloaded or shared result cards are separate copies that you control. We do not currently collect item responses for a norming study.

Hosting, access and correspondence

Hosting and security providers may process network information such as IP addresses, requested URLs and technical logs to deliver and protect the site. A private preview may additionally require its hosting provider’s sign-in. That access system is separate from the assessment. If you email us, your message, address and any attachments are processed to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

Optional analytics

On auroriq.com, accepting cookies permits Google Analytics 4 (G-50T6XM1Z3P) to measure usage such as page visits, device/browser information and referral information. Google may process network and device identifiers when providing this service. We do not send assessment answers or scores as custom analytics events. Declining cookies keeps the Analytics script unloaded on subsequent visits. Analytics is disabled on this private preview.

Advertising and Google services

Our AdSense publisher identifier is ca-pub-2335913078491957. On the public site, Google's AdSense script loads when a page opens so the publisher code is available for verification. Ad requests start paused and resume only after you accept optional cookies. Loading the script contacts Google, and existing Google-domain cookies may be read; pausing ad requests does not mean that no network contact occurs. We request non-personalized ads. Ad availability also depends on Google's review and account settings.

Read how Google uses information from partner sites and Google’s ad personalization settings. Our Advertising Disclosure explains placement and editorial boundaries. Personalized advertising for visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland requires an eligible Google-certified consent platform; this site’s basic banner does not supply that certification.

Your cookie choices

Use Cookie settings to accept or decline optional cookies. You can use the assessments either way. Declining after previously accepting disables Analytics, removes accessible first-party Analytics cookies and reloads the page with Analytics unloaded and ad requests paused. This does not erase data already processed by Google or cookies held on other domains.

To remove saved test data and preferences, clear this site’s cookies and storage in your browser. This resets your cookie choice and may permanently remove local progress. Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on is another available control.

Retention and privacy requests

Local data remains under your browser’s control. Google and hosting providers apply their own configured retention periods; we do not promise a single retention period for every provider. Email correspondence may be retained while resolving a request and for necessary records. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data, or to object and complain to a relevant authority. Contact us to make a request. The absence of an assessment account does not mean that all technical data is anonymous.

Children and sensitive information

AurorIQ is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit a child’s personal information by email or use the tools to make clinical decisions. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so the concern can be investigated.

External links

Personica and other linked websites operate under their own privacy notices. Opening an external link or sharing a result with another platform is your choice. Review that service’s practices before providing information.

Contact and changes

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@auroriq.com. This notice will be updated when practices change; the date above identifies the latest revision.
