The AurorIQ Library

Understand the science of intelligence.

Plain-language guides to IQ, cognitive domains, and what scores actually mean, grounded in published research rather than hype.

How this library works

Intelligence is one of the most over-mythologized subjects on the internet, which is exactly why this library exists. Every guide here is written to a single standard: say what the published research supports, say where it's contested, and never dress up a finding to make a score feel more dramatic than it is. The collection is organized into four tracks — start with the fundamentals if you're new to the subject, or jump straight to score ranges if you've just received a result and want to know what the number means.

Guides are written and maintained by the team behind the AurorIQ adaptive test, drawing on the same psychometric literature the test itself is built on — you can read about that foundation on our methodology page.
