About this instrument

What this measures — and what it doesn't

  • Measures

    Habit design strength

    How well your habit is set up across five factors research links to formation: a consistent cue, low friction, frequent repetition, an immediate reward, and fit with your identity.

  • Method

    20-item agreement scale

    Four statements per factor, half worded toward weak setups and reverse-scored, so simply agreeing with everything won't inflate your result. Scores are normalized per factor and averaged.

  • Result

    Strength + fixes

    An overall design-strength score and band, a breakdown across the five factors, and specific fixes for your two weakest — the levers likely to help most for the least effort.

  • Limits

    Honest limits

    It scores design, not destiny. Self-report reflects your read of your own setup, and formation still takes time and repetition. It's a mirror and a checklist, not a prediction or a guarantee.

Questions

Before you begin

What does the habit analyzer measure?

How well a habit you're building is designed to stick, across five factors research links to habit formation: a consistent cue, low friction, frequent repetition, an immediate reward, and fit with your identity. It scores your setup, not your willpower — which is the part you can actually change.

How long does it take to form a habit?

There's no magic number. A well-known study found a median of about 66 days to automaticity, but the range was enormous — from roughly 18 days to over 250, depending on the habit and person. Good design shortens the odds and the struggle; it doesn't put a fixed date on it.

Does a high score mean the habit will stick?

It means you've set it up well, which genuinely raises your chances — but design isn't destiny. Life disruptions, competing demands, and plain repetition over time all still matter. Treat a high score as good odds and a low score as a clear map of what to fix, not a prophecy.

Is my data saved anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. The habit you name and your answers never leave your device and nothing is stored or sent to a server. Refreshing the page clears everything.
