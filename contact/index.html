Get in touch

Contact us.

Have a question about your result, our methodology, or something else entirely? We'd love to hear from you.

How to reach us

The best way to contact the AurorIQ team is by email:

contact@auroriq.com

We aim to respond within 48 hours on business days. For complex technical or methodology questions, it may take a little longer so we can give you a thorough answer.

What we can help with

Questions about your result: if something about your score, percentile, archetype, or confidence interval doesn't look right, let us know. Include a description of the issue and, if possible, a screenshot of your result page.

Methodology and research: we welcome questions, corrections, and feedback on how our adaptive engine works, the research we cite, or the limitations we've disclosed on our methodology page. If you spot an error, we genuinely want to know.

Privacy and data: questions about how we handle data are covered on our privacy policy page, but if anything is unclear, email us and we'll clarify.

Partnership and press inquiries: if you're a researcher, educator, or journalist interested in cognitive testing tools, reach out and we'll get back to you.

Bug reports: if the test fails to load, a page looks broken on your device, or something doesn't behave as expected, please tell us what browser and device you're using so we can reproduce the issue.

What we cannot help with

AurorIQ is an educational self-reflection tool, not a clinical instrument. We are unable to provide medical or psychological advice, interpret your result in a clinical context, or recommend whether you should seek a professional assessment. If you have concerns about cognitive function, please consult a licensed psychologist or neuropsychologist in your area.

We also cannot provide documentation for Mensa applications, educational accommodations, employment screening, or any legal proceeding. Those purposes require a supervised assessment with a validated clinical instrument such as the WAIS-IV or Stanford-Binet 5.

About AurorIQ

AurorIQ is an independently operated cognitive assessment platform built by Muhammad — a developer with a focus on psychometrics, data-driven tools, and honest reporting. The project grew from a genuine interest in how intelligence is measured, how item response theory works in practice, and how online tools can be built to inform rather than flatter. For more on the people and principles behind the test, visit our about page.
