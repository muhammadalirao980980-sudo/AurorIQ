About this instrument

What this measures — and what it doesn't

  • Measures

    Evidence-based technique use

    How well your habits align with five techniques cognitive science supports — retrieval practice, spaced practice, interleaving, elaboration, and self-regulation. These work for everyone; no "learning style" required.

  • Method

    20-item frequency scale

    Statements about how you actually study, rated by frequency. Effective habits score for you; popular-but-weak ones like rereading and cramming are reverse-scored and count against your alignment.

  • Result

    Alignment + focus areas

    An overall alignment score and band, a breakdown across the five techniques, and specific next steps for your two weakest areas — the changes likely to help most for the least effort.

  • Limits

    Honest limits

    Self-report reflects what you think you do. Technique is a multiplier on effort, not a replacement, and outcomes depend on the subject and your background. This is a guide to better habits, not a grade or a prediction.

Questions

Before you begin

What does this study habits test measure?

How well your habits align with the study techniques cognitive science actually supports: retrieval practice, spaced practice, interleaving, elaboration, and self-regulation. Popular but weak habits like rereading and cramming count against your score, so it rewards what works, not what feels productive.

Isn't it better to study in my own learning style?

That's a common myth. Decades of research have failed to show that matching instruction to a supposed visual, auditory, or kinesthetic style improves learning. What reliably helps is using effective techniques — the ones this test measures — which work well for everyone regardless of any 'style'.

Does a high score mean I'll get better grades?

Better technique genuinely helps, but it isn't the whole story. Grades also depend on effort, time, prior knowledge, and the demands of the subject. Think of good habits as a multiplier on the work you put in, not a guarantee — and a low score as a map of the easiest wins available to you.

Is my data saved anywhere?

No. The entire test runs in your browser. Your answers never leave your device and nothing is stored or sent to a server. Refreshing the page clears everything.
